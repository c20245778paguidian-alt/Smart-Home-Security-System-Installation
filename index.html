Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay a mandatory monthly subscription?

No. Unlike traditional alarm companies that tie you to 3-year contracts, our hardware works natively via local storage or self-managed cloud options. Optional VIP support retainers are available if you want ongoing technical assistance.

How long does a typical installation take?

Most standard residential installations (3-4 cameras, smart lock, video doorbell) are completed within 3 to 5 hours on the same day.

Can I integrate my existing smart home devices?

Yes! During setup, our technicians consolidate compatible existing Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Alexa, or SmartThings accessories into your new unified app dashboard.
